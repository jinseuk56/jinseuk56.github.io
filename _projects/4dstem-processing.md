This repository collects processing and visualisation tools for four-dimensional scanning transmission electron microscopy (4D-STEM) data. The documented workflow covers radial-distribution-function analysis, fluctuation electron microscopy, multislice simulation, electron ptychography, differential phase contrast, symmetry STEM, and cepstral STEM.

It provides a practical environment for exploring local and medium-range structural variation in complex materials from multidimensional diffraction datasets.

## Related research
