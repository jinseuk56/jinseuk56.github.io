This research develops a data-driven EELS-tomography strategy for three-dimensional nanoscale field mapping. Feature extraction separates spectroscopic components before reconstruction, enabling the analysis of spatially overlapping plasmonic signals in a tilt series.

The associated open workflow is available through [CAETomo](https://github.com/jinseuk56/CAETomo).

## Publications

- J. Jo, **J. Ryu**, *et al.*, [Direct three-dimensional observation of the plasmonic near-fields of a nanoparticle with circular dichroism](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.4c10677), *ACS Nano* (2024).
- J. Jo, *et al.*, [Comprehending nonmonochromatic plasmonic behavior of metal nanoparticles on dielectric nanowires using monochromated EELS](https://doi.org/10.1103/ybgk-2mc8), *Physical Review Materials* (2025).
